# Restructure Skills

Use this route for merges, splits, ports, retirements, removals, material purpose changes, or ownership redesign. The target is a better future system, not a rearrangement that preserves predecessor files.

## Design the future ownership first

Establish the future user outcomes, entry states, directly useful owners, specialist boundaries, state and mutation authority, loading model, and credible failure classes before adapting predecessor material.

Treat one clear job as an owner rule, not a one-method-one-surface rule. For each proposed visible owner, prove a recognisable user-facing area that is clearer than direct selection through an existing owner.

A substantial method should normally become a directly selectable conditional workflow when users enter through the same area, the parent can select it from the requested result, and the selected route loads no siblings. The workflow may still contain specialist judgement, mutate its own artifacts, and own durable state or lifecycle rules. Those are workflow responsibilities unless their authority must be independent of the parent.

Keep a standalone owner only when the complete route shows a real independence boundary, such as:

- users naturally enter through another area or several unrelated areas;
- the capability has no truthful, recognisable parent without inventing a vague umbrella;
- the parent would need to overclaim expertise or mutation authority merely to route it;
- independent assessment must be selected outside the producing area's authority; or
- direct routing, cross-domain use, or total loaded context is materially better as a standalone owner.

Do not accept “it has state,” “it mutates files,” “it is high stakes,” “it is specialist,” or “it has its own lifecycle” as a conclusion. Show why those responsibilities cannot remain inside one complete directly selected workflow, as they commonly do for product records, test suites, architecture artifacts, and other area-owned outputs.

For each proposed conditional workflow, prove that the parent gives uncertain users a natural entry while explicit method requests select it immediately and it remains complete when chosen. Compare realistic end-to-end loading and handoffs; fewer surfaces do not prove lower context cost. Do not merge toward a count or split merely because a file is long.

Inspect affected owners, neighbours, resources, tools, routes, state, recurring documents, consumers, live frontmatter, and portability. For a port, separate the durable user outcome and domain judgement from platform-specific contracts and project assumptions.

Read [registration and consumers](registration-and-consumers.md). Read [platform compatibility](platform-compatibility.md) for ports or metadata and invocation changes. Read [worker use](worker-use.md) only if worker design changes. Read [external skill discovery](external-evidence.md) only when requested or local evidence cannot resolve a material target choice.

## Implement from the target

Draft directly usable future owners and conditional workflows before moving text. Keep one canonical source for each instruction, state type, document contract, and generated fact. Preserve stable external contracts only when the future outcome still needs them.

After the target is coherent, compare predecessors and other evidence to detect omitted judgement, safeguards, durable preferences, compatibility needs, resources, triggers, and failure handling. Accept an old behavior only when the future outcome still needs it and it remains a good mechanism. Remove obsolete duplication, ceremony, and project leakage deliberately.

Update canonical owners, consumers, current descriptions, named modes, and links together. Retire or remove an owner only after its continuing capabilities, consumers, state, and routes have a deliberate destination or are confirmed no longer needed. Test the underlying natural requests for every removed capability against the successor's current area trigger and direct modes. If routing fails, fix that current route at its source; do not preserve an alias, successor registry, or stale name merely for compatibility. Keep lineage project-local only when it has current migration, recovery, or evidence value.

## Review the finished system

For every newly created or materially redesigned owner, read [Pressure-test a skill](pressure-test-skill.md) and run its stages in order. Apply authorised in-scope corrections through this restructure route and rerun the affected stages.

Walk direct, vague, explicit-mode, retired-capability intent, neighbouring, stateful, incomplete-input, and failure-prone requests. Check that conditional routes load one selected depth, direct use needs no hidden preflight, and the result retains all judgement and safeguards the target outcome requires.

Use a comparison artifact only when scale, consequence, or residual uncertainty makes one useful. Complete when the future owners are coherent and better for the approved outcomes—not merely shorter, structurally tidy, or faithful to their predecessors. Stop while any material fitness doubt remains.
