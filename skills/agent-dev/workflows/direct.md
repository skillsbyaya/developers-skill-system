# Direct Delivery

Use this workflow for one clear, bounded change that can be implemented and verified inline. An existing story or package remains authoritative but does not by itself require a deeper route.

Read [Delivery controls](../references/delivery-controls.md) before editing and follow them inline, without a worker. When the selected unit is a packet within a multi-packet story or package, also read [Packet coordination](../references/packet-coordination.md); its continuation and safety gate apply even on this route.

A finished small change within an open batch is checkpointed, not landed.

If discovery exposes coupled packets, material uncertainty, a critical domain, several review slices, or durable continuation need, stop before broad edits, preserve the current record and evidence, and select coordinated or staged delivery.
