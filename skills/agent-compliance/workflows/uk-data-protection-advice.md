# UK Data-Protection Advice

Answer a substantive UK GDPR or PECR question directly. Apply current official authority to the actual processing and finish with a practical position, confidence, and any record or control consequence.

## Establish the question

Use supplied context and current scope-matched applicability findings. Establish only facts that could change the answer, such as the processing purpose, data and people involved, jurisdiction, relationship, recipients, transfer destination, scale, monitoring, or live deadline. State a bounded assumption when safe; ask one short question when the missing fact materially changes the rule or risk.

Read [the UK data-protection issue map](../references/uk-data-protection-notes.md) only for the topics involved. It is not legal authority.

## Current-law gate

Before giving a material legal conclusion, verify it against current official ICO guidance and, where the precise law or commencement matters, legislation.gov.uk or GOV.UK. Distinguish:

- law currently in force;
- regulator guidance;
- good practice;
- contractual or assurance expectation; and
- enacted, announced, or proposed change not yet in force.

Do not infer commencement from Royal Assent, a press release, or the bundled notes. Cite current official support near each substantive conclusion.

## Answer

1. Identify the rule and topic the question turns on.
2. Give the direct answer, then apply it to the processing and facts.
3. Recommend a position and make the trade-off, evidence, uncertainty, and review trigger visible.
4. State whether the conclusion requires a named privacy record, a compliance-register entry, technical work, or no further action.

A request that also authorises a DPIA, RoPA, retention, lawful-basis or consent review, or subject-access process may continue to [UK data-protection records](uk-data-protection-records.md) after the legal conclusion is established. Obtain fresh authority only when the advice reveals a material choice, record scope, accepted risk, or mutation that the original request did not establish.

Select this owner's regulatory horizon scan when the primary question is what changed or what is coming next. Select its obligations-register workflow for a canonical obligation, risk, control, evidence, or accepted-risk entry. Route technical controls to their technical owner.

Give practical guidance, not legal advice. Flag contested, high-stakes, cross-border, or genuinely uncertain positions and recommend a qualified solicitor or the ICO where appropriate.
