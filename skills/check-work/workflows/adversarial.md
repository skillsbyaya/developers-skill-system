# Adversarial Review

Pressure-test a plan, proposal, argument, decision, specification, or other bounded artifact. Assume it may be careless, overconfident, or blind to important constraints, then validate every candidate finding so aggressive posture does not become fabricated criticism.

Resolve the supplied artifact or clear conversation context. If multiple plausible targets would materially change the review, ask one short question; if no usable target exists, ask for it and stop. A requested focus adds an attack surface but never replaces independent review. This mode is for broad hostile challenge, not sustained dialogue, code or writing review, or systematic path enumeration.

## Pass 1 — hostile discovery

Try hard to prove the artifact unfit. Derive attack surfaces from its purpose and inspect relevant:

- unsupported assumptions, claims, and false precision;
- contradictions, missing dependencies, and unstated constraints;
- ignored actors, incentives, failure modes, and second-order effects;
- infeasible steps, weak evidence, premature certainty, and unsafe sequencing;
- unnecessary complexity, ceremony, or scope; and
- consequences that would materially affect the intended decision or outcome.

Generate candidate findings freely. Do not stop at a quota and do not invent one.

## Pass 2 — candidate disproof

For every candidate, try to defeat it using the artifact and relevant supplied context. Keep it only when it is in scope, not already handled, supported by specific evidence or a precise omission, and consequential enough to affect the decision. Merge shared causes and discard taste, padding, speculation presented as fact, and objections with no material consequence.

Order surviving findings by consequence. For each give a concise finding, evidence or precise omission, realistic consequence, uncertainty where material, and the smallest corrective direction without applying it. Zero findings is valid only after both passes; name the important attack surfaces that survived without claiming perfection or coverage beyond the reviewed scope.
