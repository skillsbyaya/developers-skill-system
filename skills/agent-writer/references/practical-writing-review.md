# Practical-Writing Review

Review structure before prose because sentence edits should not be made against sections that may move or disappear.

## Structure

Check whether:

- the opening gives the main point soon enough
- the order supports what the reader must understand, decide, or do
- claims, evidence, interpretation, recommendation, risks, uncertainty, and next steps are distinguishable
- important context is missing, buried, duplicated, premature, or out of scope
- headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, examples, and summaries reduce effort
- actions, decisions, owners, dates, and dependencies are clear where relevant

Recommend cuts only when material does not serve the reader or purpose. Do not treat warmth, examples, orientation, or a useful summary as waste merely because they add length.

## Prose

Flag only issues that materially affect clarity, credibility, tone, pace, or actionability:

- vague or unsupported claims, importance language, hype, jargon, or corporate filler
- unclear actors, references, cause and effect, risks, qualifications, or requests
- inconsistent terminology, unnecessary repetition, overloaded sentences, or unfocused paragraphs
- manipulative, defensive, blaming, falsely certain, or audience-inappropriate framing
- em dashes or boldface in normal body text

Skip code, frontmatter, and structural markup unless the writing depends on them. Return to the workflow report contract.
