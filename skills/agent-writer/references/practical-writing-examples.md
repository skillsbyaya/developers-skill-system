# Practical Writing Examples

Read only when a wording choice remains unclear after applying the write-or-revise workflow.

## Vague to specific

Before:

> The proposal supports future growth.

After, when the evidence supports it:

> The proposal adds a paid tier, simplifies checkout, and targets renewal churn.

## Hype to evidence

Before:

> The team has delivered outstanding progress.

After:

> The team reduced the backlog from 420 cases to 95 in six weeks.

## Corporate to human

Before:

> The organisation has demonstrated positive commercial momentum while navigating operational constraints.

After:

> Sales have improved, but technical debt and staffing gaps still slow delivery.

## Generic risk to clear risk

Before:

> The transition carries several risks that will need to be managed.

After:

> The main risks are downtime during cutover, a late contractor handoff, and data loss if the backup fails.

## Forced warmth to specific warmth

Before:

> We are delighted to share this exciting update with our valued customers.

After:

> The new dashboard goes live on 4 June. Thank you to everyone who tested the early version.
