# Product Consultation

Use this workflow when product judgement or a decision is the result, including MVP cuts, scope shaping, prioritisation, stakeholder alignment, and choosing whether another product artifact is justified.

## Frame the decision

1. State the decision in one sentence and identify who must decide it.
2. Establish the current product promise, target users, desired outcome, constraints, evidence, and commitments already made.
3. Identify the smallest set of uncertainties that could change the recommendation. Ask one focused question at a time only when the answer is material.
4. Compare viable options against user value, strategic fit, evidence strength, cost of delay, reversibility, dependencies, and delivery risk. Do not use numeric scoring when a direct trade-off is clearer.
5. Recommend one position with its rationale, assumptions, exclusions, and the signal that should cause reconsideration.

For an MVP, preserve the smallest end-to-end outcome that proves or delivers the product promise. Cut polish, breadth, automation, variants, and premature scale before cutting the core user outcome or evidence needed to evaluate it. Distinguish “not now” from “not part of this product.”

For priority advice without a requested state change, do not reorder the backlog. Return the recommended ordering or decision rule. A request to apply, groom, or sequence backlog priority selects backlog planning directly.

Finish with the decision reached, unresolved evidence, and the next owner only when another action is actually needed. A consultation does not require a brief, PRD, PRFAQ, epic breakdown, or implementation handoff.
